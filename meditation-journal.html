Add to Home Screen
You are already in Safari! Just follow these steps:
1
Tap the Share button
It is at the bottom right corner of your Safari screen — the icon that looks like a box with an arrow pointing up.
▢ ↑ Share
2
Tap “Add to Home Screen”
Scroll down a little in the menu that appears and tap this option.
✚ Add to Home Screen
3
Tap “Add” to confirm
The app icon will appear on your home screen. Open it anytime — no internet needed, no Claude needed.
